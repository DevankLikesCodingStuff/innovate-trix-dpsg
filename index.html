ii. the ritual

wash as you are.

"treat people with kind hair."

this isn't a routine. it's a small, soft ceremony. seven minutes between you and the rest of the day where the water is warm, the light is gold, and you don't have to be anyone in particular.

we made six bottles for six different mornings. fig & neroli for the day that needs softening. saffron oil for the night you want to glow without saying anything. rose & smoke for the stories you keep wrapped in silk on the nightstand.

tresemmé has been in your mother's shower for seventy-five years. we know hair. harry knows feeling. golden hour is what happens when the formula learns to listen.

"the hair you wear / like a feeling / is the hair you came in with."

no gendered aisles. no shouting label copy. no 47-step ladder of chemistry-lab claims. just six pearl bottles, a refillable promise, and a playlist on the back of every one.
